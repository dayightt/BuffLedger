# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Buff bar that sorts buffs into colour-coded categories: one per class plus Weapon, Consumable, World and Other.
- Buffs are placed by a built-in list, then by the caster's class, and always by your own choice first.
- Shift-click any buff to move it to another category; right-click cancels it.
- Consolidation: collapse a category into one icon with a count, and hover it to see the full set. Per category, all at once, or only in a group or raid.
- Categories page: rename, recolour, reorder, create, hide and delete categories, assign buffs by name, and review the assignment history.
- Separate debuff bar with borders coloured by dispel type.
- Weapon enchants with time left and charges.
- Keeps updating in combat.
- Sits exactly where the default frames were, and moves with Edit Mode or by unlocking and dragging.
- Per-bar settings for icon size, spacing, category gap, icons per row, border thickness, scale, growth direction, background, and duration placement.
- `/bl test` preview, `/bl scan` for unrecognised group buffs, and an optional minimap button.
